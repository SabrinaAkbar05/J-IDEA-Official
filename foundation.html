The Beginning

How J-IDEA Was Born

J-IDEA was created to provide students of Jinnah University for Women with a dedicated platform to present their creativity, ideas, and technical skills through engaging university activities and competitions.

The idea began with discussions among students and faculty members of the Department of Computer Science and Software Engineering, aiming to organize an event that encourages participation, teamwork, and innovation within the university environment.

J-IDEA 2026 represents a student-centered initiative focused on learning, creativity, and confidence-building through design, technology, and interactive experiences.

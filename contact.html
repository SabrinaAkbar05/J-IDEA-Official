Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in J-IDEA 2026?+
J-IDEA 2026 is open to undergraduate and graduate students enrolled at any Pakistani university. Both individual and team entries (up to four members) are welcome. All HCI, UX, and interface design projects are eligible regardless of department.
What is the registration deadline?+
Registration opens in May 2026 and closes at the end of July 2026. We strongly recommend registering early as slots are limited and workshops fill up quickly.
Is there a registration fee?+
Registration for J-IDEA 2026 is completely free for all student participants. Our goal is to make high-quality design competition accessible to students across Pakistan, regardless of their institution's resources.
How do I submit my project?+
All project submissions are handled through the official J-IDEA portal at j-idea.vercel.app. You will need to upload your project files, a short description, and any supporting documentation by the August 2026 deadline.
Are there prizes for winners?+
Yes! J-IDEA 2026 offers awards across multiple categories including Best UX Design, Best Accessibility Solution, Best AI-Assisted Interface, and an overall Grand Prize. Details on prizes and awards will be announced in June 2026.
Can I attend as an observer or guest?+
Absolutely. The Grand Finals and Award Ceremony in October 2026 will be open to guests. Faculty, industry professionals, and students are all welcome. Details on guest registration will be shared closer to the event.

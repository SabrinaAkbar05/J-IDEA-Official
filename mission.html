✦ Our Purpose ✦

Our Mission

J-IDEA exists to bridge academic learning with professional design practice — giving students a real stage, real competition, and a community that lasts beyond graduation.

Our Mission

Empowering Student Innovation

J-IDEA bridges the gap between academic learning and professional design practice, providing a platform for students to showcase their HCI projects in a competitive yet supportive environment.

J
IDEA 2026

At J-IDEA, we believe that great design emerges from collaboration, experimentation, and real-world application. Our competition encourages students to push boundaries, learn from peers, and develop skills that will define the future of human-computer interaction.

By integrating AI-assisted tools and modern design methodologies, J-IDEA prepares participants for the evolving landscape of technology and user experience design.
